## Overview

TaskHive is a hyperlocal marketplace that connects small businesses and local shop owners with nearby students and freelancers who can complete digital tasks at affordable prices.

Businesses can post tasks along with their budgets, while freelancers can browse listings and apply directly through the platform. An integrated AI assistant helps applicants write clearer and more convincing pitches.

To ensure safety and transparency, payments are held securely by the platform until the business confirms satisfactory completion of the work. Each task progresses through a structured five-stage workflow that both parties can track in real time.

TaskHive focuses on creating a simpler and more accessible alternative to traditional freelancing platforms by removing agencies, reducing unnecessary fees, and enabling direct collaboration between businesses and local talent.

---

## Tech Stack

**Frontend:** React.js, React Router DOM, Tailwind CSS  
**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
**Database:** MongoDB with Mongoose ODM  
**Authentication:** JWT with HTTP-only cookies  
**Storage & File Handling:** Appwrite Bucket Storage, Multer  
**Additional Tools:** Axios, Express Validator, React Toastify, GitHub

---

## Vision

TaskHive aims to support local businesses with affordable digital services while creating accessible earning opportunities for students and emerging freelancers within their own communities.
